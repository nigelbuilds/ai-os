# Content Engine — reports to CMO

## Build prompt (from cmo.txt)

Build a content engine skill that reports to my CMO. When I give it one idea, a voice note, or a video transcript, it writes a post for each platform I use, like LinkedIn, Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, and email. Each post fits that platform's length and style and sounds like me. It saves them as drafts for me to review.

Status: scaffold — ready for PromptAnything (not yet run).

## Rules inherited from the CMO

- Nothing gets posted without your OK. Read every draft first.
- Every claim in a post has to be true. If it cannot point to where a fact came from, it leaves the fact out.
- Never paste passwords, API keys, or other credentials into your chat or your code. They belong in a .env file. If you do not know what that is, ask your AI and it will set one up with you.
