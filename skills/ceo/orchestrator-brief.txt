YOUR AI CEO: THE ORCHESTRATOR
The router at the top of your AI company. Build it first, so every
executive you add has someone to report to. Test it last, once it has
executives to route to.

Your COO, CFO, CMO, CSO, CRO, CTO, and CCO will all report to it. You
are the owner. You tell the CEO what you need and it picks the route.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 1. BUILD THE CEO
------------------------------------------------------------------

Paste this into the PromptAnything chat box:

Build an orchestrator skill for my AI operating system. It is the CEO
of my AI company. My COO, CFO, CMO, CSO, CRO, CTO, and CCO will report
to it, and each of them will have agents under them. When I make a
request, work
out what I am trying to accomplish, then choose the most direct route
through my executives and their agents to complete it, whatever the
task is.

------------------------------------------------------------------
THEN, IN PROMPTANYTHING
------------------------------------------------------------------

1. Click the blue button at the bottom right to go to the next stage.
2. It should choose agent or skill. Push next.
3. You are on the main prompt builder. Fill in your profile once from
   the navigation menu and every prompt you build will use it.
4. Under advanced settings, set temperature closer to zero. Routing is a
   precision job, not a creative one.
5. Click refine prompt. Read every clarifying question and fix any
   answer that is off. Anything in brackets means the AI was not sure,
   usually because your profile is still empty.
6. Click generate prompt, then copy it.

------------------------------------------------------------------
THEN, IN VS CODE
------------------------------------------------------------------

Type this and paste the generated prompt after it:

  Create a new skill in my .agent/skills folder called orchestrator.md
  that does this. Every executive I add later reports to it:
  <paste your prompt>

Hold off on testing. Build your executives next, then come back.

------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP 2. AFTER YOUR EXECUTIVES, BUILD ITS AGENTS
------------------------------------------------------------------

These three report straight to the CEO because they pull from every
seat, so build them once your executives are in place. Run each one
through PromptAnything the same way. Then in VS Code:

  Create a new skill in .agent/skills/ that reports to my orchestrator
  and does this:
  <paste your prompt>

MORNING BRIEF

Build a morning brief skill that reports to my orchestrator. Each
morning it asks each of my executives for their update: what came in,
what is due, and what needs me. It turns them into one page I can read
in two minutes, with the three things that need me today at the top.

WEEKLY PRIORITIES

Build a weekly priorities skill that reports to my orchestrator. Every
Monday it looks at my goals, my pipeline, my cash, and my projects,
then picks the three moves that will matter most this week and explains
why in one line each. It also tells me what to say no to.

INVESTOR UPDATE

Build an investor update skill that reports to my orchestrator. Once a
month it pulls the real numbers from my CFO and CRO, plus wins and
problems from my other executives, and drafts a short update for my
investors, partners, or board: highlights, numbers, challenges, and
asks. It never makes up a number, and I approve it before it goes out.

------------------------------------------------------------------
TEST IT LAST
------------------------------------------------------------------

Once your executives are built, the CEO has somewhere to send work.
Ask it:

  What should I focus on this week?

Watch which executives it pulls in. If it picks the wrong one, tell it
why and ask it to update its own routing rules.

------------------------------------------------------------------
HOW IT SHOULD THINK
------------------------------------------------------------------

  A money question      -> CFO -> Bookkeeper or Cash Forecast
  A sales call tomorrow -> CSO -> Lead Researcher
  A new post            -> CMO -> Content Engine -> Brand Kit
  A new client signed   -> CCO -> Client Onboarding

Every job gets its own skill. That keeps each one focused, so the work
comes back sharper and you spend less on tokens.
